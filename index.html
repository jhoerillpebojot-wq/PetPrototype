Scan a receipt - free
Take a photo and FINUITY reads the total, store and date for you. Your photo is read right on your phone - it is never uploaded or saved.
Because this reader is free, it has limits:
• Works best on flat, well-lit, printed receipts
• Blurry, faded, crumpled or handwritten ones may be misread
• The first scan downloads a small reader (~10 MB), so it needs internet once
• Always check the amount, date and store before you save
